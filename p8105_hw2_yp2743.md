About this dataset:

The dataset originally contained records of NYC subway entrances and
exits, including line, station name, routes served, entry permission,
vending availability, and so on. I cleaned the column names to lower
case, retained the specified columns (line, station, name, station
latitude / longitude, routes served, entry, vending, entrance type, and
ADA compliance), and converted column `entry` from a character variable
to a logical variable. The resulting dataset has 1,868 rows and 19
columns. It is not yet completely tidy because route names are spread
across multiple columns, from route1 to route11.
